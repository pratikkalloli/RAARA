# Generic Academic Policy Assumptions

**Dataset:** RAARA generic academic-policy demonstration, version 1.0.0  
**Provenance:** `PROJECT_AUTHORED`  
**Source type:** `GENERIC_PROJECT_POLICY`  
**Official university policy:** `false` for every record

These values are deliberately chosen, transparent project defaults for arithmetic and retrieval demonstrations. They are not universal academic standards, PES University regulations, or requirements for an actual programme, campus, student, or admission batch. Actual institutions may use different rules and definitions. The separate PES FAQ discovery record stating 80% attendance is not overwritten or used to justify these generic values.

| Setting | Illustrative value | Project rationale and limitation |
|---|---:|---|
| Minimum attendance | 75% | Chosen as a simple demonstration threshold that supports percentage and shortfall arithmetic. It is not a universal requirement; institutions may set different thresholds, calculations, exemptions, or eligibility conditions. |
| Internal assessment | 40% | Chosen to demonstrate weighted totals. Actual assessments may use different components and weights. |
| End-semester examination | 60% | Complements the illustrative 40% internal component to make a 100% weighted score. It is not an official assessment rule. |
| Overall passing score | 40/100 | Chosen for simple pass/fail examples. This dataset defines no additional separate examination minimum; actual institutions may apply different or additional requirements. |
| Grade-point scale | 10 points | A familiar numeric range for demonstrating grade mapping and weighted averages. The sample table is wholly project-authored; actual grade bands, points, and rounding may differ. |

## Sample grade bands

The demonstration maps integer percentage scores 90–100 to 10 points, 80–89 to 9, 70–79 to 8, 60–69 to 7, 50–59 to 6, 40–49 to 5, and 0–39 to 0. These bands exist only to make sample SGPA/CGPA calculations reproducible. They are not taken from a university document.

## Numerical conventions

- Attendance = attended classes ÷ classes held × 100; display to two decimal places.
- Weighted course total = internal score × 0.40 + end-semester score × 0.60, with each component on a 0–100 scale.
- SGPA and CGPA use credit-weighted grade points, with included courses/terms explicitly shown in each example.
- No numerical rule is supplied for medical exemptions, special attendance relaxation, separate examination minimums, supplementary attempts/dates, revaluation deadlines, add/drop dates, promotion criteria, or credit-to-contact-hour conversion.

Any real-policy replacement must come from an authorized, applicable institutional source and remain subject to the separate human authority review workflow. Do not promote these records into the official registry or change their provenance labels.
