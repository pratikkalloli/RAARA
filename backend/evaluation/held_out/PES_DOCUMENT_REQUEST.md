# Request for Official Academic Regulations and Related Documents

**Draft for the project owner to send through an authorized PES University contact or document-request channel. This request has not been sent.** No recipient address is supplied here because one has not been verified for this request.

## Subject

Request for current B.Tech CSE (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) academic regulations and examination documents

## Draft

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am requesting official copies, or authorized download links, for the academic regulations and examination rules applicable to the PES University B.Tech programme in Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning). The materials are being requested for accurate academic-policy reference and research documentation.

Please provide the current documents and, where applicable, versions for relevant admission batches, covering:

1. Current B.Tech AIML academic regulations.
2. Regulations applicable to each relevant admission batch, including the batch-to-scheme mapping.
3. ISA and ESA assessment rules, including components, weights, eligibility and applicable exceptions.
4. Attendance requirements and examination eligibility regulations, including calculation, condonation and exceptions.
5. Grading rules and SGPA/CGPA calculation, grade mapping and rounding rules.
6. Backlog, repeat-course and supplementary examination rules.
7. Revaluation, answer-script review and examination grievance rules.
8. Course registration, add/drop and course withdrawal rules.
9. Academic progression, promotion, prerequisites and credit requirements.
10. Official amendments, circulars, clarifications and supersession notices affecting any of the above.

For each document or link, please identify:

- The issuing authority and approving body, if different.
- Publication date, effective date and document version.
- Applicable programme, campus and admission batch(es).
- Whether the document is current or superseded, and which document/version it supersedes.
- The amendment history and any related circulars or transitional provisions.

PDF copies or authorized university download links are preferred. If a rule is published in multiple documents, please identify the controlling document and any documents that must be read together. Please also indicate if any requested item is unavailable or restricted and the appropriate authorized process for obtaining it.

Thank you for your assistance.

Sincerely,

Project owner / requester
