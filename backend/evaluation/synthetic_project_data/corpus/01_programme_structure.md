## Programme structure

The project-model B.Tech programme has eight semesters. A standard regular-entry student completes 24 credits in each semester, for a standard total of 192 credits over eight semesters.

Ring Road and Electronic City follow the same academic rules in this synthetic project model.

The 192-credit total describes the standard regular-entry structure. Diploma lateral-entry students enter in Semester 3 and do not complete Semester 1 or Semester 2 credit requirements in this model. Their bridge-class requirements are described separately.
