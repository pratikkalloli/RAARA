## Entry and bridge classes

Diploma lateral-entry students join the project-model B.Tech programme in Semester 3. They are not required to complete Semester 1 or Semester 2 credits.

Mandatory additional Mathematics bridge classes cover foundational Mathematics knowledge from Semesters 1 and 2. The bridge classes are additional learning requirements and do not create Semester 1 or Semester 2 credit requirements in this synthetic model.

The lateral-entry progression threshold is counted from Semesters 3 and 4 only. Do not apply the regular-entry credit threshold to lateral-entry students.
