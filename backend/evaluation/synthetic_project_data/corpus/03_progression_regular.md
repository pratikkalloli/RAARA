## Regular-entry progression

For regular-entry students, the project-model progression review occurs after Semester 4 results. A regular-entry student must have earned at least 72 cumulative credits after Semester 4.

A regular-entry student below 72 cumulative credits after Semester 4 must restart from Semester 3 under this synthetic project model. This threshold applies only to regular-entry students.
