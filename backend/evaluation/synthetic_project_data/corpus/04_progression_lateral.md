## Lateral-entry progression

For diploma lateral-entry students, the project-model progression review occurs after Semester 4 results. A lateral-entry student must have earned at least 24 cumulative credits from Semesters 3 and 4.

A lateral-entry student below 24 credits from Semesters 3 and 4 is subject to the corresponding lateral-entry year-back rule. The threshold is not 72 credits: the regular-entry threshold does not apply to this category.
