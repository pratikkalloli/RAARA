## Subject attendance and ESA eligibility

The minimum attendance requirement is 75% in each subject. Attendance is checked separately for each subject.

A student below 75% attendance in a subject is normally not eligible to write the ESA for that subject.

Medical certification may be considered as an exception only when the applicable college requirements are satisfied. The Chairperson handles approval. Medical certification does not automatically grant ESA eligibility; the required review and approval must still occur.
