## Credit-weighted calculations

The project default calculates SGPA as: SGPA = sum(Course Credits × Grade Points) / sum(Course Credits).

CGPA is calculated as: CGPA = sum(Semester SGPA × Semester Credits) / sum(Semester Credits).

## Worked examples

For a 4-credit course at grade point 9 and a 3-credit course at grade point 8, SGPA = (4 × 9 + 3 × 8) / (4 + 3) = 60 / 7 = 8.5714, or 8.57 when rounded to two decimal places.

For Semester 1 SGPA 8.0 with 24 credits and Semester 2 SGPA 9.0 with 24 credits, CGPA = (8.0 × 24 + 9.0 × 24) / (24 + 24) = 8.5.
