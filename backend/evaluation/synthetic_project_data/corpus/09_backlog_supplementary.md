## Failed subjects and supplementary attempts

A failed subject becomes a backlog. A student may attempt a supplementary examination for a backlog subject.

The maximum grade available through a supplementary examination is A. When a backlog is cleared, the original F is replaced by the new grade for calculation purposes. The supplementary attempt remains identifiable in the academic record.

This project model defines no fixed maximum backlog count. Year-back eligibility is determined by the applicable credit-progression requirements, including the distinct regular-entry and lateral-entry thresholds.
